A NOTE ON THE NUMBERS

Football is complicated.
So is probability.

This is a simplified, data-fitted estimate—not the official nfl4th model or a guarantee. It uses 2022–2024 nflverse play-by-play to approximate conversion rates, field goal success, and nflfastR win probabilities. Decision outcomes are simplified; teams, weather, injuries, penalties, halftime kickoff possession, and overtime are not modeled. Extreme situations may be unreliable. The 2025 held-out evaluation is documented in the project README.

The percentages are separate conditional estimates, so they don’t add to 100%. See the model & assumptions ↗   About nflverse ↗
